Mold-specific intelligence, end to end.

Warm, humid rooms are mold's ideal habitat. Most dehumidifiers see one number, humidity. MoldGuard reads the full picture, humidity, temperature, exposure duration, and virtual dew point margin, adds weather foresight, and responds on its own.

01

Mold Risk Index engine

Real-time risk scoring built from humidity, temperature, and sustained exposure duration, with a virtual surface dew point calculation, going beyond the single-threshold triggers used by commercial dehumidifiers, and avoiding false triggers during temporary humidity spikes.

02

Environmental & VOC monitoring

A VOC sensor adds supplementary air-quality context alongside humidity, temperature, and duration. We treat VOC as context, never standalone proof: research shows microbial VOC levels are often too low for reliable detection and easily confounded by household sources.

Built from parts you can trust.

Open, off-the-shelf components, expand a row for the plain-language spec.

Humidity & temperature sensing
SHT41 sensor module, high-accuracy temperature and relative humidity measurement for the risk engine's core inputs.
VOC / air-quality sensing
SGP40 VOC sensor, supplementary air-quality context, read alongside humidity, temperature, and duration, never as a standalone mold verdict.
Dehumidification unit
A Gaabor 2-in-1 semiconductor dehumidifier (1 L tank) is switched safely by an SSR-40DA solid-state relay: the ESP32's low-voltage logic controls 220 V AC mains with strict electrical isolation. A VL53L0X time-of-flight sensor and magnetic reed switch monitor tank fill and seating.
Brain & connectivity
ESP32-WROOM-32 (dual-core, WiFi + Bluetooth) publishes telemetry and receives commands over MQTT via HiveMQ Cloud, on a pipeline built to stay stable under fluctuating network conditions.
Enclosure & placement
Sensors and controller mount in a compact ABS housing (1×200×300 mm boards) attached to the dehumidifier, with a removable 1 L tank you empty when the app tells you it's full.
Software stack
React + Tailwind web dashboard on Vercel, React Native mobile app, Node.js/Express + TypeScript backend on Render, PostgreSQL (Neon) history store, MQTT via HiveMQ Cloud, and Socket.IO real-time sync to clients.

01 SENSE

Sense the room, continuously.

A compact sensor pod watches the conditions mold actually cares about: relative humidity, temperature, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). Humidity and temperature feed the risk engine's core inputs. VOCs add context, read together with everything else rather than as a verdict.

02 ANALYZE

Score the risk, not just a number.

The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist, instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That's what makes the prediction early: it catches the window where mold can start, before it does. Outdoor forecast data sharpens that window further.

03 RESPOND

Act: dehumidify, no hands.

When the index crosses the risk threshold, MoldGuard switches the connected dehumidifier on and pulls moisture back down, closing the loop without you touching anything. Extracted water collects in the removable 1 L tank, monitored so you're told before it's full.

04 PROTECT

Report: see everything, anywhere.

Live status and full history live in the app and web dashboard. The moment risk rises, you get an alert, and the dehumidifier has already responded. You can step in with manual override from anywhere, any time.

Straight answers.

What is MoldGuard?
A compact device that watches a room's humidity, temperature, and air quality, scores the real mold risk, and runs a paired dehumidifier for you. A web dashboard and a mobile app put the full picture in your pocket.
How does it detect mold risk?
The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist, instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That is what makes it early: it flags the window where mold can start, before it does.
Does it control a dehumidifier?
Yes. MoldGuard works with its paired semiconductor dehumidifier: the risk engine switches it on and off automatically. The system pairs with the Gaabor dehumidifier specified in the quote.
What does the app show?
Every room on one screen: risk state, temperature, humidity, and whether the dehumidifier is running. Alerts arrive the moment risk rises, and manual override lets you take control from anywhere whenever you want to.
Can I monitor more than one room?
Yes. The dashboard supports multi-room and multi-unit views, so larger households, dormitories, or property managers can watch every space side by side.
How do I set it up?
Place the unit near a humidity-prone area, connect it to your WiFi, and the loop starts running. Installation support and documentation handover are part of the deployment.
What maintenance does it need?
Periodic emptying of the 1 L water tank (you get an alert before it's full) and a dedicated power source: that's the honest list, straight from our own comparison table.
Does it work without the internet?
The device buffers readings locally when connectivity drops and syncs once reconnected; the dashboard, app, and history services run hosted, so there's nothing to maintain on your machine.
How much does it cost?
MoldGuard builds its intelligence around an off-the-shelf dehumidifier with open, individually-sourced components: a fixed, itemized hardware cost, a single fixed development fee, full client ownership, and only minimal, predictable cloud service costs at small scale. Exact figures come from the team's quote, not published on the site.
Who is it for?
Renters, households, dormitories, and small property managers: anyone who wants affordable, vendor-agnostic mold monitoring without a building-management system built for towers.
