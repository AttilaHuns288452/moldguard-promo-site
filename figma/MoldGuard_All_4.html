The problem

By the time you can see mold, it has already been growing.

In the Philippines, indoor air averages 30°C and 80% relative humidity. Mold colonizes when surface humidity stays above 75%, and at 80% RH, the dew point is 26.2°C. Any wall or surface below that temperature condenses moisture silently, feeding mold you won't see for weeks.

Features

Mold-specific intelligence, end to end.

At 30°C and 80% RH, the Philippines is mold's ideal habitat. Most dehumidifiers see one number, humidity. MoldGuard reads the full picture: humidity, temperature, exposure duration, VOCs, and virtual dew point margin, and responds on its own.

FEATURE 01

Mold Risk Index engine

Real-time risk scoring built from humidity, temperature, and sustained exposure duration, going beyond the simple threshold triggers used by commercial dehumidifiers. Uses VTT/ASHRAE cumulative risk algorithms to avoid false triggers from temporary humidity spikes.

consider a small risk-dial/gauge illustration here , this is the flagship feature.
FEATURE 02

Early mold detection

A VOC sensor flags microbial off-gassing (mVOCs), chemical gases emitted by active fungal growth, before it becomes visible or noticeable by smell. Distinguishes mold signals from household chemical vapors (paints, cleaners) to reduce false positives.

FEATURE 03

Closed-loop auto-dehumidification

Once risk thresholds are met, the Peltier-based dehumidifying unit activates automatically. No manual intervention required.

FEATURE 04

Web dashboard & analytics

Historical trend logging for humidity, temperature, and VOCs, with multi-room and multi-unit views for larger households, dormitories, or property managers.

FEATURE 05

Mobile alerts & control

Real-time push notifications as risk rises, live device status, and manual override of the dehumidification unit from anywhere.

BONUS

Predictive pre-dehumidification

Weather API integration pre-dries your space ahead of incoming monsoon fronts, acting before humidity spikes, not after.

Under the hood

Sensible hardware, no black box.

Open, off-the-shelf components, expand a row for the plain-language spec.

Humidity & temperature sensing
SHT31-D sensor (DHT22 as the budget alternative), ±2% RH accuracy, ±0.3 °C temperature accuracy, full 0–100% RH range.
VOC / air-quality sensing
BME680 (recommended) or MQ-135 (budget option), detects the volatile organic compounds associated with microbial growth.
Dehumidification unit
TEC1-12706 Peltier thermoelectric module (12 V, up to 92 W heat transfer) with aluminum heatsink and 40 mm fan for hot-side heat dissipation, switched via relay or MOSFET driver.
Brain & connectivity
ESP32-WROOM-32 (dual-core 240 MHz, WiFi + Bluetooth) on 2.4 GHz WiFi, with onboard flash buffering readings during connectivity loss and syncing once reconnected.
Enclosure & placement
Compact housing (approx. 15×10×12 cm, ABS or 3D-printed PLA) with ventilated intake/exhaust slots, a removable condensate tray (~50–100 ml), and a wall-mount bracket or tabletop base.
Software stack
Next.js + React + TypeScript web dashboard, Flutter or React Native mobile app, Node.js/Express backend, PostgreSQL history store, WebSocket real-time sync.

Sense, the room, continuously.

A sensor array watches the three conditions mold actually cares about: relative humidity, temperature, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs), the microbial off-gassing that signals growth has already begun, ahead of any visible sign.

2

Score, risk, not just a number.

The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist, instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That's what makes the prediction early: it catches the window where mold can start, before it does.

3

Act, dehumidify, no hands.

When the index crosses the risk threshold, the compact Peltier-based dehumidifying unit switches itself on and pulls moisture back down, closing the loop without you touching anything. Extracted water collects in a removable tray you empty occasionally.
