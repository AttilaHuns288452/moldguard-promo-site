Mold-specific intelligence, end to end.

At 30°C and 80% RH, the Philippines is mold's ideal habitat. Most dehumidifiers see one number, humidity. MoldGuard reads the full picture, humidity, temperature, exposure duration, and virtual dew point margin, adds weather foresight, and responds on its own.

Feature 01 · flagship

Mold Risk Index engine

Real-time risk scoring built from humidity, temperature, and sustained exposure duration, with a virtual surface dew point calculation, going beyond the single-threshold triggers used by commercial dehumidifiers, and avoiding false triggers during temporary humidity spikes.

RH + TEMP + DURATION · VIRTUAL SURFACE DEW POINT · NO SPIKE TRIGGERS

Feature 02

Environmental & VOC monitoring

An SGP40 VOC sensor adds supplementary air-quality context alongside humidity, temperature, and duration. We treat VOC as context, never standalone proof - research shows microbial VOC levels are often too low for reliable detection and easily confounded by household sources.

F·03

Predictive weather integration

Weather API forecasts are combined with indoor readings to anticipate humidity spikes and pre-dry spaces ahead of them.

F·04

Closed-loop auto-dehumidification

When the index crosses the risk threshold, the ESP32 switches a connected semiconductor dehumidifier on via a solid-state relay, and off once conditions return to safe range.

F·05

Water-tank monitoring

A time-of-flight sensor and magnetic reed switch track the removable tank's fill level, alerting you before overflow.

F·06

Web dashboard & analytics

Historical trend logging for humidity, temperature, and VOCs, with multi-room and multi-unit views for larger households, dormitories, or property managers.

F·05

Mobile alerts & control

Real-time push notifications as risk rises, live device status, and manual override of the dehumidification unit from anywhere.

Proactive, not reactive.

Weather forecast integration pre-dries your space ahead of incoming monsoon fronts, acting before humidity spikes, not after.

Sensible hardware, no black box.

Open, off-the-shelf components, expand a row for the plain-language spec.

Humidity & temperature sensing
SHT41 sensor module, high-accuracy temperature and relative humidity measurement for the risk engine's core inputs.
VOC / air-quality sensing
SGP40 VOC sensor, supplementary air-quality context, read alongside humidity, temperature, and duration, never as a standalone mold verdict.
Dehumidification unit
A Gaabor 2-in-1 semiconductor dehumidifier (1 L tank) is switched safely by an SSR-40DA solid-state relay: the ESP32's low-voltage logic controls 220 V AC mains with strict electrical isolation. A VL53L0X time-of-flight sensor and magnetic reed switch monitor tank fill and seating.
Brain & connectivity
ESP32-WROOM-32 (dual-core, WiFi + Bluetooth) publishes telemetry and receives commands over MQTT via HiveMQ Cloud, with local buffering during connectivity loss and sync once reconnected.
Enclosure & placement
Sensors and controller mount in a compact ABS housing (1×200×300 mm boards) attached to the dehumidifier, with a removable 1 L tank you empty when the app tells you it's full.
Software stack
React + Tailwind web dashboard on Vercel, React Native mobile app, Node.js/Express + TypeScript backend on Render, PostgreSQL (Neon) history store, MQTT via HiveMQ Cloud, and Socket.IO real-time sync to clients.
