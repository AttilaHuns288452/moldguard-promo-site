1 Stage 1 · Sense

Sense the room, continuously.

A sensor array watches the three conditions mold actually cares about: relative humidity, temperature, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs), the microbial off-gassing that signals growth has already begun, ahead of any visible sign.

±2% RH · ±0.3 °C · VOC / IAQ

2 Stage 2 · Score

Score the risk, not just a number.

The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist, instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That's what makes the prediction early: it catches the window where mold can start, before it does.

RH + TEMP + SUSTAINED DURATION · NOT ONE THRESHOLD

Closet 82% RH 26h sustained Dew point margin −0.4°C
3 Stage 3 · Act

Act: dehumidify, no hands.

When the index crosses the risk threshold, the compact Peltier-based dehumidifying unit switches itself on and pulls moisture back down, closing the loop without you touching anything. Extracted water collects in a removable tray you empty occasionally.

AUTO ACTIVATION · APP OVERRIDE · REMOVABLE TRAY

4 Stage 4 · Report

Report: see everything, anywhere.

Live status and historical trends stream to the web dashboard over real-time sync; the mobile app pushes an alert the moment risk rises, and lets you override the unit manually from anywhere.

Do I still need a dehumidifier?
MoldGuard's built-in unit handles the prevention loop itself: it dehumidifies when risk rises, automatically. [Team note: confirm scope vs. standalone dehumidifiers before publishing — messaging.md Q3.]
What maintenance does it need?

Does it work without the internet?
The device keeps buffering readings locally when connectivity drops and syncs once reconnected. [Team note: confirm offline behavior of automatic dehumidification with firmware: messaging.md Q1.]
