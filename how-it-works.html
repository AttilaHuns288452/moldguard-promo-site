How it works

From invisible risk to handled — automatically.

MoldGuard runs a continuous loop: monitor the room, score the real mold risk, and act before conditions turn into growth.

Palma: this loop diagram is the page's centerpiece — final rendering (illustrated icons, animation, static) is yours. Keep the four stages; the loop closing back on itself is the core story ("closed-loop" from the proposal).
1

Sense — the room, continuously.

A sensor array watches the three conditions mold actually cares about: relative humidity, temperature, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) — the microbial off-gassing that signals growth has already begun, ahead of any visible sign.

±2% RH humidity accuracy ±0.3 °C temperature accuracy VOC / IAQ sensing
SENSOR ARRAY ILLUSTRATION — device cross-section showing SHT31-D + BME680
RISK SCORE UI MOCK — gauge climbing from Low → Elevated → High with trend line
2

Score — risk, not just a number.

The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist — instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That's what makes the prediction early: it catches the window where mold can start, before it does.

Fact-check: deliberately does NOT state a numeric scale, formula, or specific threshold values — the algorithm is the team's proprietary work and exact parameters aren't published in the proposal (messaging.md claim C2, rule R3).
3

Act — dehumidify, no hands.

When the index crosses the risk threshold, the compact Peltier-based dehumidifying unit switches itself on and pulls moisture back down — closing the loop without you touching anything. Extracted water collects in a removable tray you empty occasionally.

Automatic activation Manual override in the app Removable condensate tray
DEHUMIDIFICATION ILLUSTRATION — unit active, moisture drawn down, tray visible
DASHBOARD + PHONE MOCK — live status, trend charts, push notification banner
4

Report — see everything, anywhere.

Live status and historical trends stream to the web dashboard over real-time sync; the mobile app pushes an alert the moment risk rises, and lets you override the unit manually from anywhere.

Real-time WebSocket sync Historical trend charts Multi-room / multi-unit view

Setup & placement

Plug in, connect, done.

A

Place the unit

Tabletop or wall-mounted, near humidity-prone areas — bathrooms, closets, basements. Compact housing fits where mold actually starts.

B

Connect to WiFi

The device joins your 2.4 GHz network and syncs to the dashboard — readings buffer locally during any connectivity loss and catch up automatically.

C

Watch the loop run

From here it's automatic. Check trends in the dashboard, get alerts in the app, empty the tray when it's full.
