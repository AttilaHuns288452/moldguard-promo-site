1 Stage 1 · Sense

Sense the room, continuously.

A sensor pod watches the conditions mold actually cares about: relative humidity, temperature, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). The SHT41 measures the risk engine's core inputs; VOCs add supplementary context, read together with everything else rather than as a verdict.

SHT41 RH + TEMP · SGP40 VOC CONTEXT

2 Stage 2 · Score

Score the risk, not just a number.

The Mold Risk Index combines humidity, temperature, and how long those conditions persist, instead of reacting to a single humidity threshold. That's what makes the prediction early: it catches the window where mold can start, before it does. Outdoor forecast data sharpens that window further.

RH + TEMP + SUSTAINED DURATION · NOT ONE THRESHOLD

Closet 82% RH 26h sustained Dew point margin −0.4°C
3 Stage 3 · Act

Act: dehumidify, no hands.

When the index crosses the risk threshold, the ESP32 switches the connected semiconductor dehumidifier on through a solid-state relay and pulls moisture back down, closing the loop without you touching anything. Extracted water collects in the removable 1 L tank, monitored so you're told before it's full.

SSR-SAFE SWITCHING · APP OVERRIDE · TANK LEVEL ALERTS

4 Stage 4 · Report

Report: see everything, anywhere.

Live status and historical trends stream to the web dashboard over real-time sync; the mobile app pushes an alert the moment risk rises, and lets you override the unit manually from anywhere.

Straight answers.

Do I still need a dehumidifier?
MoldGuard works with its paired semiconductor dehumidifier: the risk engine switches it on and off automatically. The system pairs with the Gaabor dehumidifier specified in the quote.
What maintenance does it need?
Periodic emptying of the 1 L water tank (you get an alert before it's full) and a dedicated power source: that's the honest list, straight from our own comparison table.
Does it work without the internet?
The device buffers readings locally when connectivity drops and syncs once reconnected; cloud services (MQTT broker, dashboard, database) run hosted, so there's nothing to maintain on your machine.
How much does it cost?
MoldGuard builds its intelligence around an off-the-shelf dehumidifier with open, individually-sourced components: a fixed, itemized hardware cost, a single fixed development fee, full client ownership, and only minimal, predictable cloud service costs at small scale. Exact figures come from the team's quote, not published on the site.
Who is it for?
Renters, households, dormitories, and small property managers: anyone who wants affordable, vendor-agnostic mold monitoring without a building-management system built for towers.
