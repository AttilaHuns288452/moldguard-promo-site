The problem

By the time you can see mold, it has already been growing.

Mold grows when humidity and temperature stay favorable long enough — but most homes have no way of seeing those conditions build until visible growth or odor appears.

Fact-check: the four cards below are lifted near-verbatim from proposal §2 (Statement of the Problem) — safe to verify line by line against messaging.md claims C4–C7.
01

Discovered too late

Most households only learn mold risk exists once visible growth or odor already appears.

02

Dumb triggers

Typical smart dehumidifiers react to one humidity threshold — ignoring exposure duration and temperature — so the response comes late or not at all.

03

Zero feedback

Passive moisture absorbers offer no monitoring, no data, and no automation whatsoever.

04

Priced for enterprises

Building-grade monitoring exists, but affordable, vendor-agnostic options for renters and small property owners are scarce.

The solution

Meet MoldGuard.

A compact IoT device, a web dashboard, and a mobile app — working as one closed loop: sense the risk, act on it automatically, and show you everything.
