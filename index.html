By the time you can see mold, it has already been growing.

Tropical homes spend most of the year well above the U.S. EPA's recommendation to keep indoor RH below 60%. When warm, humid air meets a cooler wall or corner, it condenses silently, and ASHRAE's moisture criteria say it's the sustained surface humidity that matters, feeding mold you won't see for weeks.
